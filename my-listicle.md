#My top 5 Animes

## #1 Black Butler (Kuroshitsuji) 🖤
![Sebastian rising from his faux death](https://64.media.tumblr.com/36c9f878e88e55bf68a24363ede08d5d/tumblr_inline_ojj7bysgpQ1tpb4it_1280.png)

I love how its suspenseful and dark, but also has lighthearted moments and Iconic charachters like **[Grell Sutcliffe](https://kuroshitsuji.fandom.com/wiki/Grelle_Sutcliff)**.

## #2 The Apothecary Diaries (Kusuriya no Hitorigoto) 💚
![Maomao tasting poison](https://static0.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/maomao-tasting-poison-in-the-apothecary-diaries-episode-6.png?q=49&fit=crop&w=266&h=350&dpr=2)

I love how it can be super humorous but also dip into **[Maomao's](https://kusuriya.fandom.com/wiki/Maomao)** darker side, especially when she's tasting for poison.

## #3 The Disasterous Life of Saiki K (Saiki Kusuo no Sai-nan) 🩷
![Annoyed Saiki](https://mediaproxy.tvtropes.org/width/1200/https://static.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pub/images/3o5u92udbxq61.jpg)

**[Saiki's](https://saikikusuo-no-psinan.fandom.com/wiki/Saiki_Kusuo)** done with everything attitude is fun and refreshing, many charachters are anime troupes taken to extreme degrees.

## #4 I Was Reincarnated as the 7th Prince so I Can Take My Time Perfecting My Magical Ability (Tensei Shitara Dai Nana Ōji Datta no de, Kimama ni Majutsu o Kiwamemasu) 💙
![Detective Lloyd](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRG0qdBTfMH62aqGdcthDexUdqpx_Zf3lzRf8493EUZKg&s=10)

This anime is mostly fun with hints of the serious plot poking through. Most of the mischief is caused by **[Lloyd's](https://reincarnated-as-the-7th-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Lloyd_de_Saloum)** insane magical strength.

## #5 Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation (Mushoku Tensei: Isekai Ittara Honki Dasu) 🤎
![Rudeus making mischief with his dad](https://anteikuanimereviews.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/paul-and-rudeus-thinking-the-same-thing-mushoku-tensei.jpeg?w=960)

This anime gets darker the further in you go, but it is fun to watch nonetheless. Especially with **[Rudey's](https://mushokutensei.fandom.com/wiki/Rudeus_Greyrat)** penchant for trouble.
